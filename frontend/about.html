About NutriTrack

Empowering individuals to take control of their health through smart, minimalist nutrition tracking.

Project Overview

NutriTrack is a modern web-based diet and nutrition management application designed to simplify daily calorie, protein, and water tracking. Built as an academic web engineering project, it prioritizes a clean user interface, responsiveness across all devices, and efficient client-side data persistence using local storage.

Core Features

1. Personalized Goal Setting: Tailor your daily calorie, protein, and water targets based on your body weight and specific fitness objectives.

2. Smart Meal Logging: Quickly log common regional and global food items or input custom nutritional values.

3. Hydration Management: Track daily water intake effortlessly with interactive progress counters.
