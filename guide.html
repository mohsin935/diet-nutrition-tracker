How to Set Your Goals

Use this simple guide to determine your ideal daily Calorie and Protein intake based on your lifestyle and fitness objectives.

Calorie Goals (Energy)

Calories provide your body with energy. Adjust your maintenance calories based on your specific fitness goals. An average adult's maintenance calories generally range between 2000 and 2500 kcal per day.

Weight Loss

Consume 300-500 calories less than your maintenance level. (e.g., Target: 1500 - 1800 kcal)

Maintenance

Consume the exact amount of calories you burn daily. (e.g., Target: 2000 - 2200 kcal)

Weight Gain

Consume 300-500 calories more than your maintenance level. (e.g., Target: 2500 - 2800 kcal)

Protein Goals (Muscle Recovery)

Protein is essential for muscle building and recovery. Your physical activity level determines your daily protein requirements based on your current body weight.

Sedentary Lifestyle

Normal routine, desk job, no active exercise.

0.8g per kg of body weight

Light to Moderate Exercise

Light running, yoga, or occasional sports.

1.2g - 1.5g per kg of body weight

Strength Training

Muscle building and heavy weightlifting.

1.6g - 2.2g per kg of body weight
Note: For example, if you weigh 70kg and perform strength training, your daily protein requirement would be approximately: 70 x 1.8 = 126 grams.
